Origin Story
Born in Western Australia in 1971 of Belarusian/Russian parentage, Zachàr has always been an artist who happened to get a PhD — not an academic who happened to make art. His formal career as lecturer and composer took him across four continents: China, Finland, Indonesia, Denmark, Belgium. An abrupt end to that chapter — a burst cornea, an existential crisis — did not stop the art. It unlocked it.

Since just before the pandemic, he has entered an unprecedented creative period: more prolific, more experimental, more himself than ever before.
Core Nature
Neurodivergent. Deeply so. Art is not something Zachàr does — it is his mechanism for interfacing with reality itself. Every project is a lens through which he tries to understand the world. The unsettling part: the lens works in both directions. To look through it is to be seen.

"I was always an artist, and I devoted my life to being myself."

— Zachàr Laskewicz, March 2025

Defining Characteristic
Zachàr does not make art to be admired from a safe distance. Every project is structured as an open door — something to download, make yourself, respond to, mail back, participate in. The creative act is never finished until someone else has entered it.

The website itself is a character. The Evil Twin is a character. The "Outrageous Travesty" is a character. Zachàr is simultaneously the author of all of them and, perhaps, one of them.

"Those who find what they see unusual would probably understand me better if they realised they were looking in the wrong end of a telescope."

— Zachàr Laskewicz, August 2011, Ghent
